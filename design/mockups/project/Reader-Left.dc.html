Parents, politicians and best-selling psychologists are increasingly anxious about how much time children and teenagers spend on screens and how it may affect their mental health. In May, the Office of the Surgeon General warned about the harms of screen use and called for a limit of two hours a day of screen time at home for children ages 6 to 18.
